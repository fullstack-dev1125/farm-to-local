Terms of Service & Seller Rules

The deal between you and Farm to Local, in plain language. The seller rules are part of it.

Last updated September 2026 · Draft for legal review

What Farm to Local is

Farm to Local is a directory. We list sellers and let buyers find and contact them. We are not a party to any sale, we don't handle payment, and we don't take possession of any product. When you buy from a seller you found here, your contract is with that seller.

Who can use it

Anyone can browse. You must be 18 or older to list, to post a neighbor share, or to message a seller. Sellers must have a US address and a working phone and email that reach a real person.

Seller rules

1. Producers only. You may list only what you grow, raise, catch or make yourself, or — for food hubs, co-ops and multi-farm brands — products where you name the producing farm on the listing. Resold, imported, wholesale-bought or dropshipped goods are prohibited.2. Be who you say you are. Your listing must use your real business name, location and contact information. One listing per business; markets get a market listing, not a seller listing.3. Follow the law that applies to you. You are solely responsible for complying with federal, state and local law for what you sell: cottage food laws, raw milk rules, USDA and state meat and poultry inspection, FDA labeling rules for supplements and cosmetics, and home-kitchen permits where they exist. Checking an attestation box on your listing is a promise to us that you do.3a. Prepared food. Home cooks may list in any state and must state on the listing whether they hold a health permit. A listing that claims a permit it doesn't hold is removed.3b. Services. Farm & Garden Services must relate to growing, raising, processing or teaching food production. Trades that your state licenses (contractors, custom-exempt processors) must hold that license and show it at verification.4. No health claims. Herbal products, tinctures and supplements may not claim to treat, cure or prevent any disease. Homemade sun products may not be described as sunscreen or carry an SPF claim.5. Keep it current. Prices, hours and availability should be accurate. Buyers see when you last updated.6. Verification. The Verified badge is granted after we review one supporting document. We may ask for more, or remove the badge, if something doesn't add up.7. Enforcement. Listings that break these rules are removed. Reselling or misrepresenting who you are closes the account permanently. We'll tell you why.

Fees and plans

Listing is free and there are no transaction fees. Pro is a monthly or annual subscription you can cancel any time; your listing stays. Partner charges a commission only on conversions attributed to Farm to Local through a coupon code, tracked link or lead form, at the rate shown in your dashboard, billed monthly. Sponsored placements for national retailers are governed by a separate advertising agreement.

Neighbor shares

A neighbor share is a free, personal offer of surplus fruit, vegetables or garden goods from your own home. Shares may not be resold and may not include prepared food. Anyone picking up does so at their own risk, and you post at your own discretion. We show a block-level location only; you choose how to share the exact spot.

Messages

Messages sent through the site go to the seller. Don't use them to spam, harass, or send anything unrelated to buying from that seller. We may review messages to enforce these terms.

Content you post

You own your photos and text and you give us a license to display them on the site, in search results, and in our emails and social posts that promote sellers. Don't post anything you don't have the right to post.

Disclaimers

The site is provided as-is. We verify sellers in good faith but we don't guarantee any product, its safety, its legality or any transaction. Verified means we reviewed a document, not that we inspected a farm. You buy at your own judgment.

Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, Farm to Local isn't liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages arising from use of the site or from any transaction between a buyer and a seller.
