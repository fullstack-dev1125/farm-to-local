About the shop

Ray and Delia Crane. Ray ran the kill floor at a USDA plant in Fredericksburg for eleven years before we bought the trailer in 2019. Delia does the cut sheets and the books. We do about 240 beef and 300 hogs a year, all within an hour of Johnson City, and we'd rather do it right than do it fast.

How we work

We arrive at first light. The animal is handled on your ground, low stress, and the carcass is at our shop the same morning. Two weeks hanging, then cut to your sheet, vacuum-sealed and labeled. Pickup in Johnson City or we drop within 30 miles for $40.

Why we do it this way

Ray and Delia Crane started this because the nearest USDA plant is 90 minutes away and booked out six months. Custom-exempt on-farm harvest means less stress on the animal, no trailer ride, and a date you can actually get.

What to expect when you buy

You get every pound back that's legally yours: cut to your sheet, vacuum-sealed, labeled "not for sale." Hanging weight is on the invoice; take-home runs about 60% of that.

Pickup & delivery

Pickup at the Johnson City shop Mon–Sat 8–5. Delivery within 30 miles is $40. Bring coolers — a beef needs about eight.

Questions buyers ask
Do I need to be there on harvest day?

Yes — someone who can point us to the animal and sign the custom-exempt form. After that you can go inside.

Can I split a beef with a neighbor?

Yes. Two cut sheets, two invoices, two pickups. We do it every week.

Is the meat USDA inspected?

No. Custom-exempt means it goes back to the owner only and is labeled "not for sale." For resale you need a USDA plant — we can point you to two.

What if the animal doesn't go down clean?

It's Ray's job to make sure it does. Eleven years on a kill floor. If anything goes wrong, you don't pay the kill fee.

How much freezer space do I need?

A whole beef is about 400 lb take-home — two big chest freezers. A hog fits in one.

Can I see the shop?

Sure — pickup hours, or message us for a walk-through before you book.

When does fall fill up?

Usually by mid-October for November and December dates. Book in September if you can.

Do you take poultry under 50 birds?

Not as a separate day, but you can join a neighbor's day — we'll connect you.
