Seller rules

Ten rules. They're what make the badge worth something.

Farm to Local is free to list because the rules are strict. Every rule below exists because a buyer got burned somewhere else. Read them once; you'll agree to them when you list.

1
You grow it, raise it, catch it, bake it or make it.

Resellers, brokers, drop-shippers and "farm-style" brands with no farm get removed. A food hub or co-op is fine if it names the farms it sources from on its page.

2
One document, reviewed by a person.

State producer registration, cottage food permit, commercial fishing license, nursery license, market vendor letter, or a business license plus a photo of your stand. We look at it, we don't store it, and we don't publish it.

3
Say exactly how you sell.

Farm stand hours, markets and booth, delivery days and radius, shipping states. "Contact us" alone isn't a way to buy.

4
Claims have to be true and specific.

"Grass-fed" means no grain, ever. "Organic" means certified unless you write "no spray, not certified." "Local honey" comes from hives you keep. We ask for the paperwork when a claim carries a certification.

5
Prices are yours, and they're current.

Update or hide sold-out items. Pages untouched for 90 days show "may be out of date" and drop below current sellers in results.

6
Buyers deal with you directly.

No cart, no escrow, no fees between you. Deposits, refunds and pickups are your terms, written on your page. We don't mediate a sale, but a pattern of unanswered messages or no-shows gets a page paused.

7
Your own photos, one square, no stock.

Photos of your animals, fields, kitchen, boat or table. No stock photos, no AI-generated product shots presented as yours, no logos of certifications you don't hold.

8
State and federal food law is on you.

Meat inspection, egg law, cottage food limits, raw milk rules, MEHKO permits — you're responsible for compliance in your state. We list; we don't sell.

9
Reviews and messages are real.

No incentivized reviews, no posting from a buyer's account, no using a buyer's number for marketing texts. Every message thread goes both ways and can be reported.

10
Featured and Partner are labeled, not hidden.

Paying for placement gets you the top of results with a ★ Featured badge. It never buys the ✓ Verified badge and never removes a report.

What happens when a rule is broken
1 · A note
Stale prices, missing hours, a vague claim — we text you what to fix. Most sellers fix it the same day.
2 · Paused
Unanswered reports, no-shows, or a claim you can't back up. Page hidden until it's fixed. Featured fees pause too.
3 · Removed
Reselling, fake documents, fake reviews, stock photos passed off as yours. Gone, no refund, no relisting.
Agree and list

Listing takes about 5 minutes. Verification usually clears in a day.
