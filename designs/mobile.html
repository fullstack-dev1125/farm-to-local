Mobile pass

The 8 screens that matter on a phone.

Most buyers arrive from a text, a stand sign QR code or a search on their phone. These are live pages, not pictures — scroll inside a phone, tap the chips, open the menu. Rules the developer should carry across every page: one sticky top bar (56 px), a 5-tab bottom bar, horizontal swipe rails instead of grids, filters and edit forms as bottom sheets, and one sticky action bar on seller pages. Everything uses assets/mobile.css, one class-based stylesheet.
