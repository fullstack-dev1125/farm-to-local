Report a listing

The Verified badge only means something if people tell us when it's wrong. Every report is read by a person within 48 hours. Sellers who resell, import or misrepresent who they are lose the listing; false reports are ignored and repeat false reporters are blocked.

What's wrong?
Reports are confidential. We never tell a seller who reported them.Send report
What happens next
  1. A person reads it within 48 hours
  2. We check the listing, the documents on file, and usually call the seller
  3. Clear violation: the listing comes down. Reselling closes the account.
  4. Unclear: we ask the seller for more and mark the badge pending
  5. If you left contact info, you hear back either way
Not a violation

A bad experience with a purchase, a price you think is high, or a seller who was slow to reply. Message them first; if they won't respond at all, that is worth reporting.

Emergency?

If someone got sick from a product, contact your local health department first, then us.
