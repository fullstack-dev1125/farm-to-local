About the ranch

We're Marcus and Elena, second-generation on 340 acres of post-oak savanna west of Austin. Our cattle move to fresh grass every day and never see a feedlot; the chickens follow them in tractors and clean up behind. We finish on grass only, dry-age 14 days, and cut to order. See more

Why we do it this way
Grass-finishing is slower — 24 to 28 months instead of 16 — and the beef tastes like the season. We think that's the point.
What to expect when you buy
Everything is frozen and vacuum-sealed. Bring a cooler on hot days. Bulk orders come with a cut sheet we fill out together.
