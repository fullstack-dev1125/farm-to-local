The ✓ Verified badge

One document. One person looks at it. That's the whole badge.

Every seller with the blue check sent us a real document that says they produce what they sell, and a person on our team read it. We don't verify quality, taste or whether the eggs were laid on a Tuesday — we verify that a farm is a farm.

What the badge does not mean

Not an inspection. We haven't visited. Health and safety inspection is the state's job and the seller's responsibility.
Not a certification. "Certified organic" or "Animal Welfare Approved" are separate — those show as Reviewed pills only when we've seen the certificate, otherwise Self-reported.
Not for sale. Pro, Partner and Featured never buy the badge. Free sellers get exactly the same check.
Not permanent. Reports are read within 48 hours. A confirmed reseller or a lapsed document loses the badge; a pattern loses the page.
Why we don't just trust the seller's word
Every other directory does, and every other directory has a "farm" that's a warehouse in New Jersey. The document is a low bar for a real producer and a high one for a reseller. That asymmetry is the point.
What if my state doesn't register producers?
A business license plus a dated photo of your operation with your stand sign or vehicle in it. Or a 10-minute video call — we offer that from the last listing step.
Do you keep my document?
No. We record the document type, the issuing body, the date we reviewed it and who reviewed it. The file itself is deleted after review.
