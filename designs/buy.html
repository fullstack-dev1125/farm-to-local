Why buy direct

The honest case, without the marketing
You know exactly where it came fromThe name of the ranch, the person who raised it, the practices they use, since when. Not a label that says "product of USA" over meat from four countries.
Fresher, because it didn't travelPicked this week, processed this month, from within 25 miles. Store produce averages 1,500 miles and a week or two in transit before it hits the shelf.
No middleman feesNo distributor, no broker, no store markup, no marketplace cut. What you pay is what the farm gets. Quarter beef from the ranch runs $12–14/lb take-home for every cut, ribeyes included.
Real answers to real questions"Is it actually grass-finished?" "What did the chickens eat?" You can ask the person who knows — by message, at the stand, on the phone. Try that at a supermarket.
Your money stays in your countyEvery dollar goes to a household in your area, not a national chain's shareholders. That's the farm that stays in business, and the market that keeps running.
Honest about what it isn'tIf a ranch isn't certified organic, the page says so. If a home cook has no permit on file, the page says so. We'd rather you know than assume.

Questions buyers ask

Do I need an account to buy?

No. Search, read, message and go. An account only saves your ZIP, your favorites and your alert settings.

Is "Verified" the same as "inspected"?

No. Verified means a person on our team confirmed the seller is registered and real from one document. It is not a farm inspection or a food-safety certification. Practices and certifications are listed separately, and we mark which ones we've seen paper for.

How do I know it's really organic or grass-fed?

Look at the tags. "USDA Organic ✓ Reviewed" means we saw the certificate. "Not certified organic" means they told us so themselves — plenty of great farms use organic practices and skip the paperwork. Ask the seller; they'll tell you.

How do I pay?

However the seller takes it — cash, card at the market, their website, Venmo, a check. Their page says which. Farm to Local never handles money and never adds a fee.

What if something goes wrong?

Report the listing and a person reviews it within 48 hours. Sellers who misrepresent, no-show or resell get removed. A money dispute is between you and the seller, like at any farm stand — but tell us, because a pattern gets them delisted.

Can I buy in bulk — a quarter beef, a CSA share?

Yes, that's half the point. Bulk sellers show deposits, cut sheets, pickup dates and freezer space needed. Message to reserve; many will walk first-timers through it on the phone.

I don't see anything near me.

We seed listings from public registries, so a thin map usually means a rural ZIP — widen to 100 miles, check the Ships-to-you page, and subscribe: you'll get an email when the first seller near you lists.
