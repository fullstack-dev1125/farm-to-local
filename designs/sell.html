Questions sellers ask

I sell meat AND make tallow AND run a butchering class. One listing or three?

One. Tick Farm, Home maker and Service provider. Your products and services all live on one page with the right badges. Buyers searching for any of them find you.

Do I need a website already?

No. Your Farm to Local page is a website — photos, products, hours, a message button, its own address. Most sellers put it on the booth sign and stop there.

What counts as "one document"?

Anything that shows you're a real, registered operation: a state ag or DSHS registration, a market vendor letter, a cottage-food permit, a business license, a photo of your booth with your name on it. If you have nothing on paper, book a 10-minute call.

I'm a home cook without a permit. Can I list?

Yes, in every state. Your page shows "Home kitchen · no permit on file" and buyers decide. Following your state's cottage-food or MEHKO rules is on you; we list, we don't inspect.

How does the weekly email work?

Every Thursday, buyers who subscribed to their area get an email of what's in season within their radius, pulled from what sellers marked in stock that week. Pro sellers are placed first; free sellers with in-season items are included as space allows.

What does Featured actually do?

Your card gets the orange ribbon and the top position on your city's results, the "more sellers near" carousel on other shop pages, and the sidebar slot — within your radius only, and only when it matches what the buyer filtered for. Max two featured per page.

Do you take a cut of what I sell?

On Free and Pro, never. On Partner, 5–10% only on sales we can track — a coupon code, a tracked link, a booking request — and you pay monthly by card or ACH. Cash at the stand is yours.

Can I leave?

Any time, one click. Your page stays up as a free listing unless you delete it. No contract, no cancellation call.
