MEHKO tracker: which California counties allow home restaurants

California lets you run a small restaurant from your home kitchen — up to 30 meals a day, 90 a week, $100,000 a year — but only if your county has opted in. This is the list, with permit counts where counties have reported them, and who's voting next. If you cook in one of these counties, your listing on Farm to Local is free.

What a MEHKO permit allows

The limits

30 meals a day, 90 a week, $100,000 gross a year (inflation-adjusted). Family can help; one non-family employee.

The food

Prepared and sold the same day. Eaten on site, picked up, or delivered by you — not by a third-party app. No high-risk techniques.

The paperwork

Annual health permit, a home evaluation, a Food Manager certificate for you, food handler cards for helpers, one routine inspection a year.

MEHKO vs. cottage food

Cottage food is the older, everywhere-in-California program for shelf-stable foods — bread, jam, granola, candy — with no hot meals. MEHKO is the restaurant version: hot food, same-day, permitted and inspected. Many home makers hold both. On Farm to Local, cottage food lives under Bakery & Sweets and Pantry & Preserves; MEHKO meals live under Prepared Food & Meals.

What about the other 49 states?

A handful of states allow home-cooked meals for direct sale under "food freedom" laws, several allow them through cottage-food expansions, and most still don't.
