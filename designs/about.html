About

A directory of the people who actually grow your food.

Farm to Local exists because the last good map of local farms was built fifteen years ago and hasn't been rebuilt since. We're a search-by-ZIP directory of verified farms, ranches, fishermen, bakers, home makers, nurseries, farmers markets and neighbors with a tree dropping fruit. We list them. We don't sell for them.

Free for producersNo listing fees, no transaction fees, no cart between you and the farm. A rancher who'd never touch a marketplace can list here in five minutes.
Verified, by peopleOne document, reviewed by a human, before the badge goes on. Resold, imported or dropshipped goods get the listing removed. That rule is the whole reputation.
Local firstEvery search starts with a ZIP and sorts by distance. Companies that ship nationally are labeled and never mixed into local results.

Why a directory and not a marketplace

Every farm marketplace of the last decade asked farmers to manage inventory, payments and shipping labels, then took a cut of every sale to pay for it. Farmers either didn't sign up or left when the fees stacked up, and buyers found empty shelves. We took the opposite bet: make listing free and nearly effortless, send buyers straight to the way the farm already sells, and earn money from the people who want reach, not from the people growing the food.

That means we don't process your payment and we don't sit in the middle of a dispute. When you buy a half beef from a ranch you found here, you're buying from the ranch. We think that's how it should be, and it's also the only version of this that farmers stick with.

How we make money, in the open

We'd rather you read it here than wonder.
Pro listings

$19/mo or $149/yr for sellers who want top placement and analytics. Optional.

Partner commissions

5–10% only on sales we can prove we sent, via a coupon code or tracked link. No monthly fee.

Sponsored placement

National retailers pay for labeled slots on the Ships to Your Door page. Never in local results.

Never

Listing fees, transaction fees, buyer fees, selling your messages or your location.
