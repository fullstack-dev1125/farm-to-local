A quarter is a mixed split of a side — you get steaks, roasts, ground and stew meat in the proportions the animal actually has. We fill out the cut sheet together so you get more ground and roasts if that's how you cook. Everything comes frozen and vacuum-sealed, labeled by cut and date. Fits a 5 cu ft chest freezer with room to spare.
